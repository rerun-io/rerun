---
title: "Blueprint edits are remembered per blueprint"
hidden: true
type: misc
---

The native viewer now remembers your edits to a dataset's default blueprint separately from your edits to other blueprints, so switching between them keeps both sets of changes.
A blueprint you haven't edited shows the version the server had when you first opened a segment of the dataset.
All segments of a dataset share that blueprint.
Blueprint edits saved by earlier versions of the viewer are kept.
