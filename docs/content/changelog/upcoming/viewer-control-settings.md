---
title: Read and change the Viewer settings over ViewerControl
hidden: true
type: misc
---

`ViewerClient.get_settings()` and `ViewerClient.set_settings()` in Python, and the `viewer_get_settings` and `viewer_set_settings` MCP tools, read and change the Viewer's app settings as JSON.
Secrets, such as the Mapbox access token, are left out.
See [Changing settings](../viewer/extend/mcp.md#changing-settings).
