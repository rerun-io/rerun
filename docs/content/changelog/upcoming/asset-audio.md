### `AssetAudio` archetype

Audio files (`.aac`, `.flac`, `.m4a`, `.mp3`, `.ogg`, `.wav`) can now be logged as-is with the new `AssetAudio` archetype, or imported by opening or dropping them into the viewer.
The audio is considered to start playing at the time it was logged.
This only defines how audio is stored: the viewer cannot play it back yet.
