### Rulers in the 2D view

The 2D view can show rulers with tick values and axis names along its top and left edge:
![2D view with rulers, tick values and axis names along its top and left edge](https://static.rerun.io/axis-lines-2d/6fd9ef7d7455e575fd792e4dfe67959223bfb8ef/full.png)

Turn them on in the selection panel, or with `rrb.Spatial2DView(rulers=rrb.Rulers(visible=True, name_x="x", name_y="y"))`.
See the [Spatial2DView reference](../reference/types/views/spatial2d_view.md).
