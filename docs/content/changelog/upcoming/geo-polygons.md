### Geographic polygons with holes

[GeoPolygons](../reference/types/archetypes/geo_polygons.md) displays filled geographic regions with holes and per-polygon colors in MapView.
Polygons support selection, highlighting, and updates on a timeline.
See the Python, Rust, and C++ example in the [GeoPolygons reference](../reference/types/archetypes/geo_polygons.md).
