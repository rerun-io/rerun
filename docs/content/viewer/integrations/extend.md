title: Extend Rerun
hidden: true
description: Extend Rerun with your own custom data or custom Viewer UI
---

You can extend Rerun in two ways:
- log [your own custom data](../../log/send-data/custom-data.md)
- [extend the Rerun Viewer](../extend/extend-ui.md) with custom panels, visualizers, and views (Rust only)

If you need an extension mechanism that Rerun does not support, head over to [GitHub](https://github.com/rerun-io/rerun/issues) and either upvote an existing issue or open a new one.
