Rerun opens MCAP files directly, converts them to `.rrd`, and decodes ROS 2 and Protobuf messages.
To record ROS 2 bags straight to `.rrd`, see [Recording ROS 2 bags as RRD](mcap/working-with-mcap.md#recording-ros-2-bags-as-rrd).
